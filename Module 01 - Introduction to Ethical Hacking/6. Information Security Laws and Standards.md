## 1. Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS)

A proprietary information security standard for organizations that handle cardholder information (debit, credit, e-purse, ATM, POS). Applies to merchants, processors, acquirers, and issuers. Failure to comply results in fines or termination of processing privileges.

**PCI DSS High-Level Overview:**
- **Build and Maintain a Secure Network:** Install firewalls; do not use vendor-supplied default passwords.
- **Protect Cardholder Data:** Protect stored data; encrypt data transmission across open networks.
- **Maintain a Vulnerability Management Program:** Use and update anti-virus; develop secure applications.
- **Implement Strong Access Control Measures:** Restrict access by business need-to-know; assign unique IDs; restrict physical access.
- **Regularly Monitor and Test Networks:** Track/monitor all access; regularly test security systems.
- **Maintain an Information Security Policy:** Maintain a policy addressing security for all personnel.

## 2. ISO/IEC Standards

Global standards established by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO).

- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022:** Framework for establishing, implementing, and continually improving an Information Security Management System (ISMS).
- **ISO/IEC 27701:2019:** Extends 27001 to include privacy management and PII (Personally Identifiable Information), implementing a PIMS.
- **ISO/IEC 27002:2022:** Outlines best practices and control objectives for critical areas like access control and cryptography.
- **ISO/IEC 27005:2022:** Provides guidelines for information security risk management.
- **ISO/IEC 27018:2019:** Code of practice focusing on protecting PII in public cloud environments.
- **ISO/IEC 27032:2023:** Guidelines for Internet security, addressing common web and cybersecurity issues.
- **ISO/IEC 27033-7:2023:** Guidelines for implementing network virtualization security.
- **ISO/IEC 27036-3:2023:** Guidelines for securing hardware, software, and services supply chains.
- **ISO/IEC 27040:2024:** Requirements and guidance for achieving data storage security.

## 3. Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)

Provides federal protections for individually identifiable health information held by covered entities (health plans, clearinghouses, providers).

- **Electronic Transactions and Code Sets:** Standardization of EDI transactions (using ASC, X12N, NCPDP).
- **Privacy Rule:** Sets national standards to protect medical records, giving patients rights to examine and obtain their records.
- **Security Rule:** Requires administrative, physical, and technical safeguards for electronic protected health information (ePHI).
- **Employer/NPI Identifiers:** Standard national numbers to identify employers and healthcare providers.
- **Enforcement Rule:** Provisions relating to compliance, investigations, and civil monetary penalties.

## 4. Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX)

Enacted in 2002 to protect the public and investors by increasing the accuracy and reliability of corporate financial disclosures and preventing accounting fraud.

**Key Titles:**
- **Title I:** Establishes the Public Company Accounting Oversight Board (PCAOB).
- **Title II:** Auditor Independence (restricts auditing firms from providing consulting to the same client).
- **Title III:** Corporate Responsibility (executives take individual responsibility for financial reports).
- **Title IV:** Enhanced Financial Disclosures (off-balance-sheet transactions, internal controls).
- **Title VIII:** Corporate and Criminal Fraud Accountability (penalties for destroying records, whistleblower protection).
- **Title IX:** White-Collar-Crime Penalty Enhancement.

## 5. The Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA)

An American copyright law implementing WIPO treaties, defining legal prohibitions against circumventing technological protection measures used by copyright owners.

- **Title I:** Implements WIPO treaties and prohibits circumvention of digital locks.
- **Title II:** Limits liability for online service providers regarding copyright infringement by their users (Safe Harbor).
- **Title III:** Allows the owner of a computer program to make copies for maintenance/repair purposes.
- **Title IV:** Miscellaneous provisions (ephemeral recordings, distance education, webcasting).
- **Title V:** Protects original designs of certain useful articles (e.g., Vessel Hull Design Protection Act).

## 6. FISMA, GDPR, and DPA

- **Federal Information Security Management Act (FISMA):** Requires federal agencies to implement agency-wide programs to provide information security. Involves categorizing systems by mission impact and selecting appropriate security controls.
- **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):** Stringent EU privacy law effective May 2018. Applies globally to any organization handling EU citizens' data. Relies on 7 principles: Lawfulness/Transparency, Purpose limitation, Data minimization, Accuracy, Storage limitation, Integrity/confidentiality, and Accountability.
- **Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA):** UK framework updating the 1998 act, operating alongside the GDPR, making provisions for data processing, law enforcement rules, and the Information Commissioner's functions.
