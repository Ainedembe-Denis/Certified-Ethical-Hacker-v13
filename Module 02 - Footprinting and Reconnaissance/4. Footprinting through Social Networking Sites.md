## 1. People Search on Social Networking Sites

Footprinting social networks involves passively gathering available information, in contrast to social engineering where attackers actively trick people into revealing info. However, social sites can act as a medium for social engineering attacks.
- **Platforms:** Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram.
- **How it Works:** Attackers search anonymously (often without registering) by name, keyword, company, school, friends, or location. 
- **Information Gathered:**
  - *Personal Info:* Name, current location, educational qualifications, photos, videos, opinions, rumors, and facts.
  - *Professional Info:* Position, organization name, company/business, phone number, and email ID.
- This information forms the foundation for targeted social engineering attacks.

## 2. Gathering Information from LinkedIn

LinkedIn connects human resources globally. It contains vital professional information (name, position, organization, location, education). Attackers use this to build attack profiles on specific employees.

**Tool: theHarvester** (https://github.com)
An OSINT tool used for open-source intelligence gathering to determine a company's external threat landscape.
- **Usage:** Used to enumerate LinkedIn to find employees of a target company along with their job titles.
- **Command:** `theHarvester -d microsoft -l 200 -b linkedin`
  - `-d`: specifies the domain/company name to search.
  - `-l`: specifies the number of results to retrieve.
  - `-b`: specifies the data source as LinkedIn.

## 3. Harvesting Email Lists

Gathering email addresses related to the target organization is a critical attack vector for later phases (phishing, brute-forcing).
- **Tools:** Automated tools like **theHarvester** and **Email Spider** collect publicly available email addresses using search engines (Google, Bing, Yahoo, Baidu).

**Example Command (theHarvester with Baidu):**
- **Command:** `theharvester -d microsoft.com -l 200 -b baidu`
  - `-d`: domain used for harvesting.
  - `-l`: limit results to 200.
  - `-b`: extract results from the Baidu search engine (or Google, Bing, etc.).

---

### Harvesting Email Lists with AI

Attackers leverage AI (like ChatGPT) to effortlessly construct tools and commands for finding valuable email details.

**Prompt Example:** *"Use theHarvester to gather email accounts associated with 'microsoft.com', limiting results to 200, and leveraging 'baidu' as a data source"*

**Generated Command:**
```bash
theHarvester -d microsoft.com -l 200 -b baidu -f Microsoft_emails.xml
```
*Explanation of flags:*
- `-d microsoft.com`: Target domain.
- `-l 200`: Limits results to 200 for manageable output.
- `-b baidu`: Uses Baidu (a Chinese search engine) as the data source.
- `-f Microsoft_emails.xml`: Saves the email accounts in an XML output file.

## 4. Analyzing Target Social Media Presence

Online services allow attackers to discover the most shared content across social media using hashtags/keywords, tracking accounts, URLs, and email addresses for phishing attacks.
- **Tools:** BuzzSumo, Google Trends, Hashatit, Ubersuggest.
- **BuzzSumo** (https://buzzsumo.com): An advanced social search engine that finds the most shared content for a topic, author, or domain across Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, Google Plus, and Pinterest. 
  - *Attacker Usage:* Track shared content related to the target domain to obtain social media account info, URLs, and email addresses.

## 5. Tools for Social Networking Footprinting

Attackers use tools to footprint sites like Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, and Pinterest to gather sensitive info (DOB, education, employment, relatives, business strategy, potential clients, project plans).

**1. Sherlock** (https://github.com)
- Searches a vast number of social networking sites for a target username, locating the user and providing complete profile URLs.
- **AI Prompt Example:** *"Use Sherlock to gather personal information about Sundar Pichai and save the result in recon2.txt"*
- **Command:** `sherlock SundarPichai --output recon2.txt`
  - `sherlock`: Runs the tool.
  - `SundarPichai`: The target username.
  - `--output recon2.txt`: Saves the results to a file.

**2. Social Searcher** (https://www.social-searcher.com)
- Allows attackers to search for content on social networks in real-time with deep analytics data.
- **Usage:** Tracks a target user on various sites to obtain complete URLs to profiles, their postings, and other personal information.
